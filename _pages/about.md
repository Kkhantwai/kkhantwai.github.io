I am an Olympiad mathematics educator based in Yangon, Myanmar, currently pursuing further studies in mathematics. Over the past several years, my work has centered on helping students transition from routine calculation to genuine mathematical thinking, proof writing, and creative problem-solving.

## Teaching & Mentorship
I teach competition and Olympiad mathematics to students ranging from elementary school through the undergraduate level. In my classes, I focus on breaking down complex problems into intuitive first principles, building strong analytical reasoning, and encouraging students to explore multiple approaches rather than memorizing formulas.

Whether preparing younger learners for international mathematics competitions or mentoring older students through foundational topics, my goal is to make rigorous mathematics both approachable and rewarding.

## Mathematical & Technical Interests
Outside of teaching, I dedicate much of my time to independent study in pure and applied mathematics with particular interests in number theory, calculus, probability, and combinatorics.
